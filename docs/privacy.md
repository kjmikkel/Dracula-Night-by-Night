# Privacy policy — Dracula Night by Night

*Last updated: 18 October 2026*

Dracula Night by Night is a free, open-source Android app for reading Bram Stoker's *Dracula* at the pace it was
written. This policy describes what the app does with information about you. In short: the app collects nothing and sends
nothing anywhere.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. It
does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

## What the app stores on your phone

To work, the app keeps a small file on your phone with your own choices: how you read (calendar, same tempo or
freely), which entries you have read and where you stopped, your bookmarks, filters and display settings. The app
never sends this file anywhere. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, or when you clear the app's storage in
Android's settings.

If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include this file in your phone's backup (for
example to your Google account), so your reading place survives a new phone. That backup is made by Android, under
your backup provider's privacy policy; you can turn it off in Android's settings.

## Saving, sharing and loading your reading

In Settings, under "Your reading", you can copy that same information to a file: save it where you choose, share
it (by email, a message or a cloud app, for example), or have the app keep one file you choose up to date as you
read. You can also load such a file to carry on where it left off. The file goes only where you send it. The app
uses Android's own file picker and share sheet to do this, and still never goes online itself. If you pick a cloud
app such as Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, that app does the uploading, under its own privacy policy. A file you
saved or shared stays where you put it until you delete it; uninstalling the app does not remove it.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses them only to tell you that an entry has arrived. It schedules them on
your phone; nothing is sent from or to a server.

## Links

The About page has links to the app's source code and to this policy on GitHub, and to the book on Project
Gutenberg. Following a link opens your browser; what happens there is covered by GitHub's or Project Gutenberg's
privacy policy, not this one.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, children included.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Questions or concerns: open an
